## AI Search Optimisation Notes (Apply to All Pages)

For pages to appear in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews:

- **Every FAQ section** must have complete, standalone answers (AI tools lift FAQ answers directly)
- **Use numbered lists** for "how to" sections — AI tools prefer structured content
- **Cite specific data** (₹20L GST threshold, 72% late payment stat, 15 million freelancers) — AI tools prefer pages with specific figures
- **Use definition boxes** for jargon terms (CGST, TDS, SAC code, LUT) — these get quoted
- **Add "last updated"** date to every page — AI tools prefer fresh content
- **No fluff in first 100 words** — AI tools index the opening paragraph heavily

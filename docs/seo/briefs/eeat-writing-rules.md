# E-E-A-T + AI Citation Writing Rules
**Apply to every page produced from the content briefs.**
Last updated: June 2026

These rules fix the gaps identified in the seo-content audit. Every writer must follow this before submitting a draft.

---

## 1. The "Who / How / Why" Test — Answer All Three

Before publishing any page, the draft must answer:

| Question | How to answer it |
|---|---|
| **Who** wrote this? | Author byline at top + author bio box at bottom |
| **How** was it created? | "Written by [name], a freelancer with X years experience in Y" — not "our team" |
| **Why** does it exist? | The page must add something no competitor covers — state it in the intro |

---

## 2. Author Bio (Non-Negotiable on Every Page)

Add this bio block at the bottom of every article. Adapt the name and role:

```
**Written by [Name]**
[Name] is a [role — e.g. "freelance graphic designer based in Bengaluru who has been
invoicing Indian clients for GST compliance since 2020"]. ClearWork is built by
freelancers, for freelancers — see our [founder story →].
```

For GST/TDS/legal pages, add:
```
*Reviewed for accuracy by [CA Name], Chartered Accountant, [City].*
```

---

## 3. One First-Hand Sentence Per Section

Every H2 section must contain at least one sentence that demonstrates direct experience. Examples:

- "When we invoiced our first inter-state client, we got the CGST/SGST split wrong — here's the rule we wish we'd known."
- "Most freelancers we spoke to had never heard of Form 16A until their CA asked for it."
- "In our experience, clients who receive a UPI payment link pay within 24 hours. Clients who receive only a bank transfer number take 7–14 days."

These sentences are what Google's E-E-A-T raters look for under **Experience**. They are also exactly what AI tools like Perplexity cite — specific, attributable, memorable.

---

## 4. Mandatory Data Points + Citations Per Page

Every page must include **at least 4 cited data points**. Use these:

| Stat | Source to cite |
|---|---|
| 15 million freelancers in India | NASSCOM / Ministry of Skill Development |
| 72% of freelancers get paid 30+ days late | (ClearWork internal or India freelancer survey) |
| ₹20 lakh GST threshold | CGST Act, Section 22 |
| GST rate 18% on professional services | CGST/SGST Rate Schedule, SAC 9983 |
| IT Act 2000, Section 5 — e-sign validity | Ministry of Electronics and IT, India |
| Section 44ADA — 50% presumptive income | Income Tax Act, 1961 |
| TDS Section 194J rate 10% | Income Tax Act, 1961, Section 194J |
| UPI processed 13.4 billion transactions in March 2025 | NPCI monthly report |

External link these citations to:
- GSTN: `https://www.gstn.gov.in`
- MeitY IT Act: `https://meity.gov.in/content/information-technology-act`
- Income Tax India: `https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in`
- NPCI UPI: `https://www.npci.org.in`

---

## 5. AI-Quotable Anchor Statement Per H2

Each H2 section should open with a single, standalone, quotable sentence. Format:

> **[Topic] in one sentence:** [Complete, specific, citable fact.]

Examples:
- "Indian freelancers providing professional services charge 18% GST under SAC code 9983."
- "A freelance contract is legally enforceable in India under the Indian Contract Act, 1872, provided both parties have consented."
- "UPI payment links embedded in invoices reduce average payment time from 14 days to under 24 hours."

These are the sentences AI Overview, Perplexity, and ChatGPT will extract and quote. Write them as complete, standalone facts — not as leads into a paragraph.

---

## 6. FAQ Format Rules (Critical for AI Citation)

Every FAQ answer must:
- Start with a **direct answer in the first sentence** — not a preamble
- Be **50–150 words** — AI tools prefer concise, complete answers
- Include the **primary keyword or close variant** in the answer (not just the question)
- End with a **related action or link** where relevant

Bad FAQ answer:
> "There are several factors to consider when thinking about GST registration for freelancers in India..."

Good FAQ answer:
> "Freelancers in India must register for GST once their annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Registration is mandatory regardless of turnover if you supply services inter-state or through an e-commerce platform. Below the threshold, registration is optional but beneficial if your clients are GST-registered businesses (they can claim ITC)."

## 7. Page Freshness Signals

Add to every page:
- `**Last updated: June 2026**` — visible, near the top
- Mention the current financial year (FY 2025-26) where relevant
- For GST/TDS pages: explicitly state "Rates and thresholds current as of FY 2025-26. Check [incometaxindia.gov.in](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in) for updates."

## 8. Trust Signals Checklist

Before publishing, tick every box:

- [ ] Author byline visible above the fold
- [ ] Author bio box at page bottom
- [ ] At least 4 external citations to government/authoritative sources
- [ ] "Last updated" date visible
- [ ] ClearWork privacy policy linked in footer
- [ ] HTTPS confirmed
- [ ] No claims without sources (especially GST rates, legal validity, TDS rates)
- [ ] For GST/TDS/legal pages: "Reviewed by CA" note added

---

## 9. Readability Rules

- **Sentence length:** max 20 words average. Split long sentences.
- **Paragraphs:** max 3 sentences. One idea per paragraph.
- **No passive voice** in H1/H2 headings ("Create a GST invoice" not "A GST invoice can be created")
- **Numbers over words:** "₹20 lakh" not "twenty lakh rupees"
- **Jargon rule:** first time a term appears (CGST, TDS, SAC, LUT), define it in parentheses immediately: "CGST (Central GST — the central government's share of the tax)"
- **Indian English:** use "crore", "lakh", "₹" not "$", "freelancer" not "independent contractor"

---

## 10. Thin Section Red Flags

Flag any section under 100 words in a 1,500+ word post. Thin sections to watch:

- "How it works" — should have 3–5 concrete steps, not a paragraph
- "Pricing" — must include the actual INR number, a comparison, and a CTA
- "FAQ" — minimum 4 questions, each 60–100 words
- "Intro" — must state the problem, the reader's situation, and what the page resolves

If a section can't reach 100 words with genuine content, merge it with an adjacent section rather than padding it.

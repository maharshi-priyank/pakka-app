## What is ClearWork?

**ClearWork** is a client management SaaS built specifically for Indian freelancers, consultants, and growing creative agencies. It replaces the cobbled-together mess of WhatsApp, Canva, Google Sheets, and UPI QR codes with one professional workflow — from the first lead to the final payment.

**Tagline:** "Find clients. Win them. Get paid."

**Hero headline (landing page):** "Find clients. Win them. Get paid."

**Hero subtext:** ClearWork is the end-to-end business platform for Indian freelancers and agencies — from finding clients to tracked proposals, e-sign contracts, GST invoices, and UPI payments, all in one place.

**Brand badge copy:** "Client CRM for Freelancers & Agencies" — ClearWork is publicly launched and generally available (no longer early access / waitlist).

**Websites:**

## Core Features

### 1. Lead CRM (Pipeline)
- Kanban-style pipeline (Enquiry → Proposal Sent → Negotiating → Won/Lost)
- Add leads from WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, or referrals
- Follow-up dates with reminders
- Pipeline value and win-rate dashboard
- Source tracking (where did the lead come from?)

### 2. Proposals
- Branded proposals with cover page, scope, pricing table, and timeline
- Real-time tracking — push notification the second the client opens it
- See which pages they read and for how long
- AI proposal drafter (powered by Gemini) — generate proposal from a brief
- One click converts to a contract
- Proposal templates (system templates + user-saved)
- Import PDF/DOCX proposals via AI parsing

### 3. E-sign Contracts
- OTP-based digital signature (legally valid under IT Act 2000)
- Auto-generated from accepted proposal — scope and price pre-filled
- Client signs from any device, no DocuSign, no printing
- Full audit log (IP, timestamp, OTP confirmed)

### 4. GST Invoices
- CGST/SGST/IGST auto-calculated by client's state (GSTIN lookup)
- TDS flagging and deduction tracking
- Payment link embedded in every invoice — UPI, credit/debit card, net banking
- Auto WhatsApp reminders at day 3, 7, 14 overdue
- Recurring invoice support
- Multi-currency invoicing (Studio plan)
- GST quarterly report export — CA-ready PDF

### 5. Client Portal
- Every client gets a white-label branded portal link
- All their proposals, contracts, and invoices in one place
- Pay directly from the portal — no login required for clients
- Gated file attachments (pay to unlock)

### 6. Automations
- Visual workflow builder (trigger → action)
- Triggers: proposal accepted, contract signed, invoice overdue, lead created, etc.
- Actions: send email, send WhatsApp message, trigger intake form
- Pre-built system automations for common patterns
- Auto onboarding flow after contract signing

### 7. Projects & Time Tracking
- Project management linked to clients
- Time entries with hourly rate tracking
- Expense tracking (billable and non-billable)
- Convert time entries directly into invoice line items

### 8. Task Management
- Kanban task boards per project
- Team assignment (Studio plan)
- Due dates and completion tracking

### 9. Revenue Dashboard
- Pipeline value (active leads)
- Monthly revenue trend
- Overdue invoice tracker
- Expense breakdown
- P&L summary

### 10. Intake Forms
- Customisable client intake forms
- Auto-triggered after contract signing
- Submissions stored per client

### 11. AI Features
- AI proposal drafter: generate full proposal from a 2-line brief
- AI lead extractor: paste a WhatsApp message or image → extracts lead fields
- AI template parser: upload a PDF/DOCX proposal → converts to reusable template
- AI chat assistant: ask questions about GST, freelancing, tax, project pricing

## User Workflow (The 6-Step Journey)

```
Lead captured  →  Proposal sent  →  Contract e-signed
     ↓                                      ↓
Follow-up set              Invoice generated (from contract)
                                    ↓
                        Client pays via payment link
                                    ↓
                         Automations handle the rest
```

1. **Capture every lead** — Add from Instagram/LinkedIn/WhatsApp. Track pipeline value and follow-up dates.
2. **Send a tracked proposal** — Branded with scope, pricing, timeline. Get notified when opened.
3. **E-sign the contract** — One click from proposal → contract. OTP e-sign, IT Act 2000 valid.
4. **Send a GST invoice** — Auto-filled from contract. CGST/SGST/IGST by state. Payment link embedded.
5. **Client gets their portal** — All proposals, contracts, invoices in one branded link. Pay from portal.
6. **Automate follow-ups** — WhatsApp + email reminders. Onboarding forms. All hands-free.

## Technical Architecture (for engineering context)

- **Frontend:** React 18 + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4 + TanStack Query v5
- **Backend:** NestJS + Prisma ORM + PostgreSQL (hosted on Supabase)
- **API hosting:** Fly.io
- **Landing page:** React + Vite, hosted on Vercel
- **Payments:** Razorpay (UPI, card, net banking)
- **AI:** Google Gemini (proposal drafting, lead extraction, template parsing, chat)
- **Authentication:** JWT with refresh tokens, global JwtAuthGuard
- **Email:** Nodemailer / transactional email service
- **Key entities:** User, Client, Lead, Proposal, Contract, Invoice, Project, Task, Expense, TimeEntry, Meeting, AutomationRule, Thread (messaging)

## Frequently Asked Questions (from landing page / support)

**Is ClearWork free?** Yes — the Free plan (up to 5 clients, 10 active projects, 3 proposals/month) is free forever, no credit card required. Pro (₹149/mo) and Studio (₹649/mo) remove the limits and add more features.

**Is GST calculation automatic?** Yes. Enter your client's GSTIN and state — CGST/SGST or IGST is calculated automatically.

**Can clients pay via UPI?** Yes. Every invoice has a payment link (UPI, card, net banking via Razorpay) built in.

**Is e-sign legally valid in India?** Yes. Our OTP-based e-signature is valid under the IT Act 2000 and the Indian Evidence Act.

**Do you take a cut of my payments?** No. We charge a flat monthly subscription. Zero transaction fees.

**Can I use ClearWork from outside India?** Yes. The app works globally. Billing is in INR but we display approximate prices in USD, EUR, GBP, and AED.

**What happens if I outgrow the Free plan?** You'll be prompted to upgrade to Pro or Studio once you pass 5 clients or 10 active projects. Your existing data is never deleted — you just can't add more until you upgrade.

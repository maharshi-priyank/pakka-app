Where should tax education live?

The invoice editor has GST type, GST %, and TDS rate fields — all bare with zero explanation. Where should users find help?

A

Inline — info icons next to each field

Every tax field (GST type, TDS %, GST %) gets a small ⓘ icon. Click it → popover explains that specific field with a plain-English definition and example. Plus a floating "Ask AI" button at the bottom of the invoice form for open questions.

GST Type i
IGST — use when your client is in a different state than you.
CGST + SGST — use when client is in the same state.
💡 ClearWork detects this automatically from the client's address.
B

Dedicated "Tax Help" side panel inside invoice editor

A collapsible panel on the right side of the invoice editor. Contains a full mini-guide: what is GST, IGST vs CGST/SGST, TDS rates, with a live AI chat box at the bottom. User can toggle it open/closed.
